Processing and retention

Forum Fortress receives configured registration, posting and other forum events to return protection decisions and customer logs. Raw submitted identifiers are retained for no more than five days. Allowed activity is deleted after that operational window and does not create persistent intelligence. Active customer logs may contain redacted or pseudonymised records.

Forum Fortress does not try to identify a real person behind an event. Retained abuse-prevention signals are isolated by signal type rather than a cross-forum identity graph. Each value in the public checker is independently checked and the checker does not reveal related identifiers, forums, content, dates or profiles.

Security and subprocessors

Access is authenticated and customer logs are tenant-scoped. The current Standard DPA records the applicable technical measures and requires the versioned subprocessor list to identify providers and locations.
